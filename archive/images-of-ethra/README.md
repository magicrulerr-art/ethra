## Counting-doctrine note (2026-08-25)
qwen-image-2.0 cannot reliably render exactly N distinct silhouettes: across
v5–v8 it produced 6, 6, 6 and 8 figures for a 7-figure brief, each time with a
different merge/omission. For crowd scenes: keep figures tiny, anonymous and
back-viewed; tolerate one EXTRA anonymous silhouette (canon-compatible, e.g.
Cefiro/Sylva are present in most company scenes) rather than chasing exact
counts; never tolerate a MISSING named figure or human drift.
