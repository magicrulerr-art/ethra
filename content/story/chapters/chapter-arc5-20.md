## Chapter 20: 12:03 — Cefiro Arrives

It was 12:03 in the afternoon. The city was chanting. The spirits were fighting. And Ajani, suspended between fire and ice, his aura draining into both, reached for the third page.

Ajani reached for the third page. The fire serpent constricted around his right arm, and the ice ring tightened around his neck, and his fur was frozen white across his shoulders, but his claws were steady. He tore the page from the grimoire—this one shimmering with a faint green light—and began to chant.

<div class="dialogue-block">
<p class="speech-line">"Spirit of light from the west, the sun that shines upon us all—warm, unforgiving, unflinching. This humble sorcerer beseeches you: take from me and answer my plea. Be made manifest and answer my plea. I call to thee—SERAPHIM!"</p>
</div>

A golden Styx materialized on his right shoulder. It was not the white male whose birth had heralded his coronation, not one of the circling females whose fires painted the sky in shades of flame, but something older—a spirit of pure light in the form of the death-from-above. Its talons sank into Ajani's shoulder, drawing blood that welled up bright against his golden fur. The light spear—the third of the five that still surrounded the creature—burst in a flash of brilliant radiance.

From that radiance stepped a golden Ajani. It was identical to the king in form and feature, but its body was made of living light, and it carried a two-handed sword of pure solar fire. It fell upon the creature without hesitation, and where its blade cut, the black flesh sizzled and smoked. The wounds did not regenerate. The light was burning something deeper than flesh—burning the Plague sorcery itself, the ancient corruption that had let the creature heal.

The light Ajani extended its hands. Twin sabers of golden flame appeared in the fire Ajani's grip. A golden scepter materialized in the ice Ajani's frozen claws. The three elemental avatars moved as one. The fire Ajani ducked low and began cutting at the creature's legs, the sabers carving through chitin and ichor with the same relentless fury that Ajani himself had shown in the tournament. The ice Ajani raised the scepter and began chanting—a sound like glaciers calving into the sea—and two of the creature's massive legs flash-froze, locked solid in casing of white frost.

Blood began to drip from Ajani's nose. It ran down his lip and chin and splashed against the golden breastplate of the Lightbringer's armor. He did not seem to notice.

<div class="dialogue-block">
<p class="speech-line">Nefere saw the blood. She was still clutching the communication shell, still leaning on her cracked staff, still bruised from the mirror array's explosion. She raised the shell and shouted into it, her ancient voice cracking with urgency. "Those in the ghosts! Repeat after me! Spirit of light from the west, the sun that shines upon us all, warm, unforgiving, unflinching! This humble sorcerer beseeches you—take from me and answer my plea! Be made manifest and answer my plea! I call to thee—SERAPHIM!"</p>
</div>

The chant spread through the ghosts, through the wall, through the temple. The Veylar Shell-Singer took it up, her melodic voice weaving the words into a harmony that resonated through the stone. The light Ajani grew. Its form swelled and intensified, matching the fire Ajani and the ice Ajani in size—nine meters of living radiance, its two-handed sword now a blazing pillar of solar fury. The fire Ajani, its twin sabers whirling, carved another leg from the creature.

The ice Ajani, its scepter raised, froze another. The creature screamed—a sound of genuine, desperate agony, its purple eyes flickering, its wounds no longer healing.
