## Chapter 4

**The Welcome**

The coils held.

Ajani braced his feet against the chitin and pulled.

The moment he put force into it, the scars the six names had left him
flared all at once. The flare drained him as it went. He pushed, and the
strength to push left with it, and what was left of him was holding a
coil he could no longer have moved against.

It spoke into his head.

<div class="dialogue-block">
<p class="speech-line">"We told you, little lord. You are wounded. Even in your prime you couldn't have been a match for us. Now even less so. Agree to our terms."</p>
</div>

*'Forcing an agreement is not what good neighbors do.'*

The answer came in images.

<div class="dialogue-block">
<p class="speech-line">"It's what strong neighbors do. And strong neighbors are the best neighbors a wounded lord can have. I'll help you this time, as a show of good faith, little lord."</p>
</div>

The golden dust that still hung around him began to crack. Golden
lightning ran through it, bright and quick, and then the dust seeped:
into him, through his fur, into the places where the scars sat.

The strength came back. All at once, and more than he had lost.

He looked at the wurm with wide eyes.

*'What?..'*

The wurm's head came down to his.

<div class="dialogue-block">
<p class="speech-line">"We are old, older than you can imagine, little lord. We have seen this ailment in our peers. You tried to put too much force into your body, and you broke it. I shared some of my strength with you. But it's not a true cure. It's merely a second skin. You need to grow stronger. You need to molt, or your current body will die. However, I'm not sure your species can molt, little lord."</p>
</div>

*'I agree to your terms. Release me, please.'*

The coils came apart.

The wurm stood, and stood tall. The cavern could not hold it whole. The
roof reached it only halfway up its body; the rest of it went up into
the dark above and was not seen.

Everyone in the cavern waited. Weapons still up, pincers still set. They
waited to see what would come.

Ajani spoke.

<div class="dialogue-block">
<p class="speech-line">"I, Ajani Bright Mane, First of my name, caller of the spirits, vanquisher of the dark creatures, the white dawn. Welcome our neighbor, Goldy, into our midst."</p>
</div>

Nikolai nodded. Cefiro nodded with him. The name sat well with both of
them. A king had welcomed his neighbor, and Goldy was a fine name.

Everyone else winced.

Anastasia went quiet.

*'It must be something in the males' blood.'*

Her eyes found her youngest son and settled on him, adoring. She hoped,
with everything she had, that he would never succumb to that particular
malaise.

Kira pressed a paw over her eyes and held it there.

Blackie and Reddy clapped their pincers together, fast and eager,
delighted with the name.

Pearl did not clap. She brought both pincers down over her face and held
them there.
