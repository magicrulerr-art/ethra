If the king accepted the egg, Seris would owe Vasha a debt. And Vasha collected debts.

The pattern is not that Tyrants are born evil. The pattern is that Convergence-born inevitably fall. The power of both suns, the weight of being touched by both the Flicker's chaos and the Steadfast's order, eventually breaks them. The madness comes for all of them. It is only a matter of time.

The First and the Fifth both fell to madness. They are also the two Tyrants most directly connected to the Rune-Belt. The First Tyrant's capital was in the desert, but his madness—the parasitic fire that consumed everything—is the signature of something that twists natural power into annihilation. Velarius went into the Flickermarch and came back changed. Something in the Rune-Belt wounded him, or woke him, or claimed him.

The Threx remember the Plague. Both Tyrants who fell to madness were touched by the thing in the Rune-Belt, and both times, the Threx suffered.

The Third Tyrant, Uthgar, is the hinge. He trained under the Deep in the marshes. He made a pact with the Tree in the desert. Two Tyrants fell to madness. Both were touched by the entity in the Rune-Belt. The Tree's hatred of the entity is still unexplained, but the Deep's hatred now has a clear source. Both weapons spiraled beyond their creators' control. Both left scars on the world that have never healed.

And neither succeeded in killing whatever they were trying to kill.

The entity in the Rune-Belt is the oldest of the super-organisms. It predates the Kyre Tree and the Mycelial Deep. It has been sleeping or waiting or growing since before the Wengari walked the desert. The Deep hates it because the Deep's children were burned twice by the weapons meant to destroy it. The Tree hates it for reasons still unknown. And two White Dawns—the First and the Fifth, both touched by the entity's influence—spent their reigns trying to annihilate it, and failed, and became monsters in the attempt.

That is why the Dragari are coming. They are the oldest race, and they carry the oldest memories. They know what the entity is and why the Tyrants tried to destroy it. Ajani is walking the same path Uthgar walked, but the entity is stirring again, and the choice between pact and weapon will soon be forced upon him.
