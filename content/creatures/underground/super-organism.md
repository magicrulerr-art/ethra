**Classification:** Tyrant-Class Entity  
**Biome:** Underground (feared across all biomes)  
**Attunement:** Unknown  

## Description

Tyrant-class beings that the Chi'Thak avoid at all costs. Depicted in vivid glowing stone murals showing each super-organism slaughtering the Chi'Thak by the millions.

## Significance

- Each super-organism represents a different Tyrant that has emerged during Convergences
- Chi'Thak murals serve as warnings and records of past predator-prey relationships
- These entities are considered apex threats even by the Chi'Thak hive

## Known Tyrants

- The Second Tyrant (Crr'zzak) — "The Fear on Wings" — originally Chi'Thak himself, evolved through Flicker's influence

## Chi'Thak Response

The Chi'Thak stay clear of these super-organisms. Their underground cities are filled with murals as a form of collective memory and warning to future generations.
