# Sand Wurm

**Classification:** Legendary Deep Wurm (arthropod)  
**Biome:** Underground (the deep desert, beneath even the oldest wurm-routes)  
**Attunement:** Unknown

## Description

A wurm of the deep places, said to reach some twenty meters — larger than any snow wurm ever bred or seen. Its chitin glows with hues of gold and green: the colors of deep places that have never seen either sun, and which turn aside even runed weapons.

## A Creature of Inference

No one has seen a sand wurm. Its existence is only inferred — by the corpses of lesser beasts left behind, and by caves found filled to the brim with bones and old husks. Whatever meets a sand wurm does not come back to say so.

## Behavior

Unknown. It moves like something that has never once needed to hurry, and those few traces it leaves suggest neither malice nor hunger that any living scholar can name.
