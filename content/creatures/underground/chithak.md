# Chi'Thak

**Classification:** Sentient Race / Anchor Species (Underground)  
**Biome:** Underground (subterranean networks)  
**Attunement:** Steadfast-attuned and Flicker-capable (rare dual-attunement)

## Physical Description

Small insect-like beings, about the size of a small cat. They possess six appendages (two for walking, all six for "earth swimming") and a pair of wings for flying.

## Caste System

- **Warriors** — Slightly larger and more formidable
- **Workers** — Smaller, white, never go outside
- **Harvesters** — The only caste other races encounter
- **Queen** — Extremely long-lived, central to colony survival

## Culture

The proverbial "blight upon the land." No one knows where they came from or how old they are, but everyone knows when they are coming.

## Survival Cycle

They emerge only during **Convergences** (the Resonance), from underground burrows. Their flight blots out the sky. They devour everything in sight. Scouts that return engorged are consumed by the colony to endure until the next cycle. No Chi'Thak ever survives two cycles — their drive is cannibalistic.

## Communication

Via **pheromones**. Hive mentality.

## The Fourth Tyrant — Crr'zzak, "The Fear on Wings"

He was taller and stronger than every other member of his race due to Flicker's influence. When he emerged, the blight came with him. He flew from end to end of the continent in weeks, devouring everything. Every current Chi'Thak owes its life to him.
