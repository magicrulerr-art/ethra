# Snow Wurm

**Classification:** Mount / Beast of Burden  
**Biome:** Umbral Ring (northern ice fields and caves)  
**Attunement:** Flicker-attuned

## Physical Description

A massive, multi-segmented arthropod native to the frozen wastes of the Umbral Ring. Snow Wurms are serpentine in shape, growing up to five meters in length, with dozens of segmented body sections each bearing a pair of sturdy legs—hundreds in total, moving in synchronized waves that give them an eerie, fluid grace. Their chitin is pale white to ice-blue, providing natural camouflage against the snow and ice of their native territory.

The royal wurm line, bred by the legendary Nikolai, possesses distinctive golden chitin and grows larger and faster than common Snow Wurms. These royals live nearly twice as long as their wild counterparts.

## Habitat and Range

Snow Wurms are found exclusively in the northern reaches of the Umbral Ring, where the cold is intense enough to sustain their unique aura adaptation. They den in deep ice caves and emerge onto the open tundra to hunt and travel. They cannot survive in warm climates—the heat of the Steadfast Desert or even the moderate temperatures of the Rune Belt would kill them within days.

## Magical Properties

Snow Wurms are **Flicker-attuned**, and their primary aura ability is a unique form of **cryogenesis**:

- **Ice Path Generation:** A Snow Wurm's aura flash-freezes the ground beneath its body, creating a smooth sheet of ice. The Wurm then glides over this ice with startling speed—far faster than its leg count would suggest. A charging Snow Wurm can outrun a galloping horse across open terrain.
- **Aura Chill:** The area immediately around a Snow Wurm drops to freezing temperatures. This passive effect keeps their ice paths stable and makes them difficult to approach for creatures not adapted to extreme cold.
- **Molting Vulnerability:** Snow Wurms molt periodically as they grow. During the molting process, their new chitin is soft and their aura is weakened, leaving them vulnerable. Snow Paw handlers guard their molting Wurms jealously.

## Behavior

Snow Wurms are surprisingly docile for their fearsome appearance. They are pack-oriented creatures with a strong herd instinct, and they respond well to consistent handling. In the wild, they travel in small groups called "glides," moving across the tundra in synchronized waves, their combined auras creating wide ice roads behind them.

They feed primarily on mineral-rich ice and the hardy lichens that grow beneath the snow, supplemented by occasional prey that wanders too close. Their metabolism is slow when at rest but explosive when moving—a Snow Wurm can burn through its energy reserves in a single day of hard travel.

## Domestication

The Snow Paws have domesticated Snow Wurms for centuries. Unlike the prestigious Sunraptor bonds (which are individual and elite), Wurm-handling is a communal skill passed down through families. A Snow Paw village's wealth is measured partly by the size and health of its Wurm glides.

**Role as Mounts and Transport:** Snow Wurms are the backbone of Snow Paw logistics. Two fully grown Snow Wurms can carry forty Wengari warriors with full provisions across the frozen tundra. Their ice-path ability allows them to traverse terrain that would be impassable for wheeled carts or unarmored feet. In wartime, they serve as rapid transport for troops and supplies, and their broad backs can be fitted with howdah-like saddles carrying multiple riders.

**The Royal Line:** Nikolai, the legendary Wurm-keeper, spent decades breeding a superior line of Snow Wurms. His "royal wurms" live ten years instead of the typical five or six, are faster, produce smoother ice paths, and possess distinctive golden chitin. They are highly prized and rarely traded outside the immediate family.

## Relationship to Snow Paws

The Snow Paws and Snow Wurms share a symbiotic bond unlike any other Wengari-mount relationship in Ethra. Snow Wurms provide the mobility that allows the Snow Paws to survive in the Umbral Ring's brutal environment; in return, the Snow Paws protect and feed the Wurms through the harsh winters. A Snow Paw without a Wurm glide is effectively land-bound—a crippling disadvantage in the endless white expanse of the north.

When the Snow Paws answer the call of the Convergence, they will ride on Sunraptor-back and Wurm-glide both. The north moves slowly, but when it arrives, it arrives in force.

## Lifecycle

Snow Wurms hatch from egg-clusters laid in deep ice caves. The hatchlings are small—no longer than a human arm—but grow rapidly, reaching full size in two years. Common Snow Wurms live five to six years; royal Wurms live up to ten. Their short lifespan is balanced by rapid breeding—a single female can produce two clutches of twenty to thirty eggs per year.

## Rarity

Common Snow Wurms are plentiful within the Umbral Ring but virtually unknown outside it. Royal Wurms are rare, with fewer than a hundred in existence at any given time. A royal Wurm is a treasure beyond price among the Snow Paws, and their bloodlines are tracked with the same care as noble lineages.
