# Sea Marsh (Veylar Cultivar)

**Classification:** Cultivated Flora / Living Marsh  
**Biome:** Umbral Ring / ancestral; primarily tended by Veylar families  
**Attunement:** Steadfast-attuned  

## Physical Description

A semi-sentient marsh cultivated by Veylar families. Each family tends their own small patch, which serves as a living repository of their bloodline's connection to the Abyssal Heart.

## Significance

- Each Veylar family cultivates a small amount
- The High Shaman is chosen by the marsh itself
- It is anathema to let it die or suffer
- Used to facilitate communion with the Abyssal Heart

## Properties

- Responding to emotional states of the caretaker
- Glows with soft bioluminescence at night
- Provides communication channels across oceanic distances
- Required for Veylar Shell-Singer magic to function

## Relationship to the Dragari Cultivar

The Veylar Sea Marsh is the **ancestral form** of the marsh Dragari families cultivate in coastal tidal pools. The Dragari version shares the same genus but is grown in smaller family patches; it is adapted to intertidal zones rather than sustained submersion.

See: `sea-marsh-dragari.md` for the Dragari sibling entry.
