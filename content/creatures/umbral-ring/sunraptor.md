# Sunraptor

**Classification:** Aerial Predator / Mount  
**Biome:** Umbral Ring (Ice City and northern territories)  
**Attunement:** Flicker-attuned

## Physical Description

Peregrine variants native to the northern territories of the Umbral Ring. Their bodies are sleek and aerodynamic, built for high-speed diving attacks. Their plumage shifts between dark grey and pale white, allowing them to blend with both storm clouds and snowfields. Their eyes are large and keen, adapted for hunting in low-light conditions.

## Hunting Behavior

Sunraptors earned their name from their primary hunting technique: they dive from great heights with the sun at their backs, using the glare to blind their prey before striking. In the Umbral Ring's perpetual twilight, they have adapted to hunt by the light of Flicker's red glow, timing their dives to the sun's faint presence on the horizon.

They are fast—faster than anything in the sky except perhaps the Styx of the Steadfast Desert.

## Relationship to Snow Paws

The Snow Paws of the Great Ice City have tamed Sunraptors as flying mounts for centuries. Peregrine-trained Snow Paws bond with individual Sunraptors from hatching, raising them as partners rather than beasts of burden. A Sunraptor and its rider share a deep bond—the birds are intelligent enough to refuse unworthy riders and patient enough to endure long flights across hostile terrain.

Cefiro, a renowned Snow Paw Peregrine, flew a Sunraptor from the Ice City across the entire Steadfast Desert to reach Styxian, a journey that took months of night-flying to spare the birds from the desert heat.

## Usage as Mounts

Sunraptors are not endurance flyers—they need to rest often. Snow Paw riders typically fly only at night to spare their mounts from heat exhaustion. During the day, rider and bird dig into the sand or snow and wait for the sun to set. They are not patient birds, but their loyalty to their riders is absolute.

## Significance

Sunraptors are one of the few aerial assets available to the Wengari. Their speed and diving attacks make them invaluable for reconnaissance and rapid assault. The presence of a Sunraptor-trained Snow Paw is a mark of high status among the northern families.
