## Physical Description

Giant sea creatures revered as "long-lost brothers" by the Dragari. Their massive bodies glide through coastal waters with grace despite their size.

## Symbiotic Relationship

The Dragari commune with them via songs and offerings. During the Crimson Tide, Dragari survival depends on securing an Auruch's protection through offerings of fish.

## Dragari Connection

- Revered as kin by the Dragari
- Communed with via traditional songs passed down through generations
- Protected by Dragari families from raiding parties of other races
- Serve as living arks during Tide events

## Significance

Without Auruch protection, the Dragari would perish during Convergences. Their songs call the creatures to shelter the Dragari beneath their massive forms in the deepest coastal waters.
