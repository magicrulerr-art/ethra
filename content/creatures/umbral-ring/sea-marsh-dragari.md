**Classification:** Cultivated Flora / Sacred Marsh  
**Biome:** Umbral Ring  
**Attunement:** Steadfast-attuned

## Physical Description

Cultivated by each Dragari family in small amounts. Unlike the Veylar's Sea Marsh, this is grown in coastal tidal pools and shallow waters.

## Significance

- Each Dragari family cultivates a small amount
- The High Shaman is chosen by the marsh itself
- It is anathema to let it die or suffer
- Used to facilitate communion with the Auruch

## Properties

- Responds to emotional states of the caretaker
- Required for proper song-casting to Auruchs
- Glows faintly with bioluminescent properties at dusk
- Connected to the coastal waters where Auruchs dwell

## Ritual Use

Dragari songs are sung to the marsh, which then carries the offering intention to the Auruch. Without properly tended marsh, the communion fails and the family is left vulnerable during Convergences.
