**Classification:** Sentient Race / Anchor Species  
**Biome:** Umbral Ring  
**Attunement:** Flicker-attuned (forcefully adapted to Steadfast)

## Physical Description

Three-meter-tall lizard-like beings with powerful arms and **iridescent scales**. Two geographic variants:
- North-dwelling: Deep green hue
- South-dwelling: Deep blue hue

## Magical Properties

Their Aura control allows them to harness oxygen from water via skin osmosis, enabling exceptional underwater capabilities. They can propel themselves at sub-sonic speeds in water, creating waves to stun and harvest fish.

## Culture

The quiet heart of the Umbral Ring — a race defined by profound, ancient gentleness. They believe everything has a soul and deserve respect. Fearful of other races, they seldom let themselves be seen.

## Symbiosis

- **Auruchs** — Revered as "long-lost brothers"
- **Sea Marsh** — Each family cultivates a small amount; High Shaman chosen by the marsh

## Unique Traits

- History is oral, handed down via songs
- Meet once every five years in rotating schedules
- Isolationist by nature and ideology
- Rarely surpass 50 years lifespan
