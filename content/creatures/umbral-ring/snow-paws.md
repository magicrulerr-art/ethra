# Snow Paws

**Classification:** Sentient Race / Wengari Family  
**Biome:** Umbral Ring (Great Ice City and northern territories)  
**Attunement:** Steadfast-attuned

## Physical Description

Tall anthropomorphic felines mimicking **snow leopards** — the fifth surviving Wengari family. Their fur is thick, white-to-pale-grey with subtle spotting, adapted to the perpetual cold of the Umbral Ring's northern reaches. Their long thick tails wrap around them for warmth. Like all Wengari, they are masters of **Aura control**, hardening fur into living armor and enhancing claws/strikes to devastating effect.

## Culture

Deeply nomadic, warlike, and xenophobic — even by Wengari standards. They respect only might and power. The northern reaches of the Umbral Ring have no agriculture; the Snow Paws survive through hunting, raiding, and sheer endurance. They view the desert-dwelling Bright Paws as softened by comfort.

**The Great Ice City:** Unlike their ancient nomadic traditions, the Snow Paws have built a permanent settlement in the far north of the Umbral Ring—a city carved from ancient ice and stone, where they gather during the harshest winters and in times of war. From this frozen citadel, Peregrine riders launch on Sunraptor-back across the continent.

**Exile History:** The Snow Paws were driven into exile by the **First Tyrant** (a Shadow Paw) during his genocidal paranoia as his second Tide approached. He mandated the genocide of entire families and often delivered the end personally. The Snow Paws fled north into the Umbral Ring's frozen wastes and survived — a point of immense pride and bitter memory.

## Magical Attunement

**Steadfast-attuned, masters of Aura control** — the same dual-nature as their Rune Belt cousins, honed by constant Tide pressure in the harsh north. They can:
- Harden fur into living armor against cold and claw
- Enhance strikes to deliver devastating blows
- Endure extremes that would kill other races

Unlike the Bright Paws, **no Snow Paw has manifested Sorcery** (hard-light constructs). This is suspected to be bloodline-linked to the Bright Paw royal line.

## Internal Politics

The Snow Paws remain a distinct family among the Wengari, nursing ancient grudges. They do not acknowledge Bright Paw supremacy easily. Their chieftains are chosen by combat and survival feats, not lineage. They have not produced a Tyrant — but the First Tyrant's genocide of their kin is the wound that defines them.

## Relationship with Other Races

- **Other Wengari families:** Tense. Shadow Paws bear shame; Bright Paws are rivals; Mottled/Stripe Paws are distant.
- **Pyrinae:** No direct contact — too far north for desert trade.
- **Dragari:** The Dragari are their neighbors in the southern Umbral Ring, but the Snow Paws rarely venture that far. They know of the gentle lizard-folk but have little interaction.
- **Threx:** Mythical; the Flickermarch is legend to most Snow Paws.
- **Hummans:** Rarely encountered. Viewed as soft merchants.
- **Veylar:** Unknown; the Tidepools are beyond their horizon.
- **Chi'Thak:** The Blight reaches even the north. They know the signs.

## Notable Lore

- **First Tyrant's Genocide:** The Shadow Paw First Tyrant unified 20 families, then turned on them. The Snow Paws were driven into exile into the Umbral Ring. This is the oldest wound in Wengari memory.
- **Convergence Cycles:** The Snow Paws endure every Tide in the open tundra. Their survival is a testament to Aura mastery.
- **Sunraptor Bond:** Snow Paws are the only Wengari family to have mastered Sunraptor taming, giving them unmatched aerial mobility.
