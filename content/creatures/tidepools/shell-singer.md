**Classification:** Veylar Specialist Caste  
**Biome:** Tidepools  
**Attunement:** Steadfast-attuned  

## Physical Description

Veylar who channel the Resonant Network through Petal-Shells — organic tools that pulse with soft, rhythmic light. Serve as the primary diplomats and magic-users of their race.

## Abilities

- Channel the Resonant Network across entire oceans
- Remote listening and transmission through Petal-Shells
- Sing old songs for communication across vast distances
- Must consult the moon and sing before making important decisions

## Role

The primary diplomats of Veylar civilization. They carry the Sea Marsh's wisdom to other races and are the only Veylar who regularly interact with surface dwellers.

## Petal-Shell

Their primary tool for communication and magic. The shell cradled in their hands pulses with soft, rhythmic light. Through it, they can project songs across entire oceans and listen to the thoughts of distant beings.

## Veylar Queen

The Veylar Queen holds immense authority and has never traveled to the surface — until recently, when she emerged to meet with Ajani during the Convergence preparations.
