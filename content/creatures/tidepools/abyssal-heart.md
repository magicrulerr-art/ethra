**Classification:** Deep Oceanic Entity / Abyssal Being  
**Biome:** Tidepools  
**Attunement:** Steadfast-attuned  

## Physical Description

A deep oceanic entity that dwells in Ethra's deepest trenches. The Veylar commune with it through Shell-Songs, receiving guidance from the abyssal depths.

## Powers

- Communicates across vast oceanic distances through the Resonant Network
- Feels the burn of Plague weapons in the water
- Guardian of the deep places beneath Ethra
- Shares knowledge of "the thing that dwells within the belt"

## Relationship to Veylar

The Veylar draw their magic through this entity. Shell-Songs channel its will across the oceans. It is their primary deity and source of oceanic wisdom.

## Role in the Convergence

Knows of the thing below the belt, sharing this knowledge only with the Kyre Tree, the Mycelial Deep, and Ajani. Its awareness stretches across the deepest places during times of celestial alignment.
