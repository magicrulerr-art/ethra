**Designation:** Sentient Race of the Tidepools

**Attunement:** Steadfast-attuned

## Overview

The **Veylar** are an extremely long-lived aquatic race whose sense of time is fundamentally unlike that of surface races. They inhabit the Tidepools and the deep reaches of the world-ocean. They have never walked the land and have no roads, no caravans, no cities beyond the Tidepools.

The Veylar are the **only race never bested by the Wengari**. Their isolation, magic, and oceanic mastery have kept them safe from the upheavals that have broken other civilizations.

---

## Physical Description

- Sleek, amphibious bodies adapted to land and sea
- Skin tones ranging from deep indigo to pale pearlescent
- Distinctive cranial ridges and elongated ear-membranes sensitive to sub-aural frequencies
- Capable of sustained submersion; surface on ceremony, the moon phases, and trade

---

## Magical Attunement

Steadfast-attuned. Veylar magic flows through two channels:

- **The Abyssal Heart** — a deep-oceanic entity the Veylar commune with directly
- **The Sea Marsh** — a semi-sentient cultivated flora, family-tended, which buffers Aura for communication

Through these conduits, Veylar Shell-Singers can project songs across entire oceans. This is the **Resonant Network**.

See `creatures/tidepools/shell-singer.md` for the Shell-Singer as a creature, and `creatures/umbral-ring/sea-marsh-veylar.md` for the ancestral Sea Marsh the Veylar cultivate.

## Culture & Governance

- **The Harmonic Council** governs through deliberation and moon counsel
- **Shell-Singers** are the primary diplomats and magic-users
- **The Veylar Queen** holds immense authority and has historically never traveled to the surface
- Isolationist but deliberate in diplomatic relations
- Their long lifespan means decisions move at the speed of tides — fast to surface races, glacial by Veylar reckoning

---

## The Veylar Tyrant — Xal'thyra

*A Shell-Singer of extraordinary power, born during a Convergence and touched by both suns. She was the first Veylar to achieve perfect Resonance with all three super-organisms simultaneously — the Kyre Tree, the Mycelial Deep, and the Abyssal Heart.*

For a century she was a healer, mediator, and scholar. She brokered peace between Veylar and Wengari, and taught the Pyrinae resonant harmonics.

Then she heard a discord in the world's song that no one else could hear — something buried deep beneath the waves, older than the super-organisms. She became convinced the only way to correct it was to silence every other voice.

**Her method was not fire or plague. It was silence.** She used her Resonance to dampen the Aura of every living thing within her reach, slowly, inexorably. Entire coastlines fell quiet. Cities of the Veylar, Hummans, and Pyrinae were reduced to ghost towns — their inhabitants alive but empty, their inner songs extinguished. She called it the **Great Stillness**.

Her city, **Vey'sul**, now stands as a wound on the world — a place of frozen time.

She silenced the male Styx and projected the Great Stillness across the world. She is the **only Tyrant to ride a Styx** and the **only Veylar to ever break the white flame to her will**.

## Climate & Geography

Coastal shallows, intertidal zones. Shallow waters where coral citadels rise from the seabed. The Veylar possess the deepest knowledge of the oceans, but even they do not claim to know all its secrets.
