**Classification:** Living Structure / Veylar Architecture  
**Biome:** Tidepools  
**Attunement:** Steadfast-attuned  

## Physical Description

Living structures grown from coral and shell, rising from the seabed in the shallow coastal waters. Home to Veylar Shell-Singers who channel the Resonant Network.

## Properties

- Grown through organic cultivation, not built
- Shimmers with soft bioluminescence matching Veylar skin tones
- Connected to the Resonant Network through embedded Petal-Shells
- Expands and contracts with tidal cycles

## Significance

Veylar have never walked the land; they have no roads, no caravans, no cities beyond the Tidepools. The coral citadels are their only architecture, grown in family clusters across the shallow waters.

## Location

Found in the shallowest areas accessible to air-breathing creatures at low tide, but fully submerged during high water. Visitors must time their approach carefully or risk drowning.
