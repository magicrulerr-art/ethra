# Motted Paw Wengari

**Classification:** Sentient Race / Wengari Family  
**Biome:** Rune Belt  
**Attunement:** Steadfast-attuned with master Aura control

## Physical Description

A Wengari family resembling jaguar-like humanoid felines. Spotted and rosetted coats for camouflage in forested areas.

## Magical Properties

Like all Wengari, masters of Aura control, capable of hardening fur into living armor and enhancing physical strikes.

## Culture

Nomadic hunters who favor the forested regions of the Rune Belt. One of the three remaining Rune Belt families alongside Shadow and Stripe Paws.

## Relationship to Rune Belt

Traditional stewards of the temperate forest zones, maintaining the balance between predator and prey in their territories.
