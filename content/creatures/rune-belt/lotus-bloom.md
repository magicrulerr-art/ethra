## Physical Description

Very rare and frail plant with delicate petals that shimmer with subtle magical energy. Grows in specific microclimates within the Rune Belt's temperate forests.

## Magical Properties

The Lotus Bloom is the key to the Tide Wolf's magical abilities. When consumed, it catalyzes specialized Aura effects in the predator's physiology.

## Lifecycle & Ecology

Its rarity creates a tight, high-stakes predator-prey dynamic. The plant blooms only during certain seasonal cycles, making it a precious and fiercely contested resource.

## Relationship to Tide Wolves

The favorite food of Tide Wolves. Without Lotus Bloom, Tide Wolves lose their most powerful magical abilities, making them vulnerable and less territorial.

## Harvesting

Attempts to cultivate Lotus Bloom artificially have largely failed, leading to continued wild-harvesting by the bravest Wengari hunters during specific seasons.
