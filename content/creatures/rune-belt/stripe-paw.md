# Stripe Paw Wengari

**Classification:** Sentient Race / Wengari Family  
**Biome:** Rune Belt  
**Attunement:** Steadfast-attuned with master Aura control

## Physical Description

A Wengari family resembling tiger-like humanoid felines. Distinctive striped coats provide excellent camouflage in dappled forest light.

## Magical Properties

Masters of Aura control, using it to harden their fur into living armor and enhance claws and strikes to deliver devastating blows. Their Aura-based abilities make them formidable in close combat.

## Culture

Nomadic hunters who favor the forested regions of the Rune Belt. One of the three remaining Rune Belt families alongside Shadow and Motted Paws.

## Relationship to Rune Belt

Traditional stewards of the temperate forest zones, maintaining the balance between predator and prey in their territories.
