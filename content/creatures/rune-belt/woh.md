**Classification:** Mount/Creature  
**Biome:** Rune Belt

## Physical Description

Large terrestrial creatures used by Wengari as mounts. Size comparable to a horse. Their bodies are built for ground-based combat and transport.

## Usage

Ridden by Wengari generals and soldiers. Multiple units of Woh riders were deployed during the battle of Styxian, showing their importance in Wengari military strategy.

## Combat Role

Woh riders form a heavy cavalry unit, capable of powerful charges and holding ground against enemy formations.

## Relationship to Wengari

Unlike flying mounts, Woh are reliable on varied terrain. They require training but are more accessible than aerial mounts.
