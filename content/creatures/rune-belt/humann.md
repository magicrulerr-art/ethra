# Humman

**Classification:** Sentient Race  
**Biome:** Rune Belt (primary), with settlements across multiple biomes  
**Attunement:** Flicker-attuned (negligible Aura)

## Physical Description

Human-like beings, by all intents and purposes identical to Earth humans. Their shorter gestation and lower Aura requirements allow for rapid population growth.

## Magical Properties

Flicker-attuned but hold negligible Aura. Not particularly attuned to Sorcery either, making them the weakest magically among Ethra's sentient races.

## Culture

Excellent merchants and politicians. They have forged alliances with every sentient race except the Threx. Their capital is **Verdantis** in the Rune Belt.

## Trade

Primary exports:
- Pearly Scorpion mounts and eggs
- Rune-glass (in partnership with Pyrinae)
- Various goods traded across Ethra

## The Fifth Tyrant — Velarius Vane

Their most recent and traumatic legacy. A 65-year plague that killed indiscriminately, with impossible immunity and excruciating death. This fresh history still haunts their culture.

## Relationship to Rune Belt

The Hummans are called "Staple Species" rather than "Anchor Species" — they arrived later and established themselves through commerce and diplomacy rather than ancient territorial claims.
