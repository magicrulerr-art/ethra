# Black Fire Tide Wolf

**Classification:** Apex Predator / War Mount (Elite)  
**Biome:** Rune Belt  
**Attunement:** Flicker-attuned (secondary Steadfast adaptation)

## Physical Description

A rare and fearsome variant of the Tide Wolf, the Black Fire Tide Wolf is distinguished by its jet-black coat, which seems to drink the light rather than reflect it. Its eyes burn with a cold, amber light, and when it moves, shadows cling to its flanks like a living cloak. Its most distinctive feature is its **shadow flare**—a dark, flickering aura that ignites around its body when it hunts or fights, burning with a cold, hungry flame that is neither fire nor shadow but something between.

## Origin

Black Fire Tide Wolves are not a separate species but a rare mutation born of the Rune Belt's volatile magical environment. They are believed to be Tide Wolves that have been exposed to concentrated Flicker energy—perhaps from feeding on Lotus Bloom that grew in a site of ancient sorcerous activity. The mutation grants them abilities beyond those of ordinary Tide Wolves, but it also makes them more aggressive and solitary.

## Magical Properties

- **Shadow Flare:** The Black Fire Tide Wolf can ignite its body in sheets of dark flame. This flame burns with supernatural intensity—it has been known to scorch through the chitin of the Blight's spawn, where ordinary fire could not. The flame is also draining; a Black Fire Tide Wolf cannot sustain it indefinitely.
- **Dark Sight:** Their amber eyes can see through magical obscurement and illusions, making them exceptionally dangerous to prey that relies on deception.
- **Silent Stride:** Their connection to shadow allows them to move with preternatural silence, even over loose stone or dry leaves.

## Behavior

Unlike the pack-hunting Tide Wolf, Black Fire variants are often solitary or found in pairs. Their aggression makes them difficult to integrate into standard packs, and they range over vast territories. They are unpredictable—even other Tide Wolves give them a wide berth.

## Relationship to Wengari

During the Great War, the elite Black Tide Wolf cavalry served as a shock unit. Led by Zephyr, a scarred Shadow Paw commander, these riders and their wolves were deployed against the Blight-spawned creature that besieged Styxian. The Black Tide Wolves surged forward, their shadow flares igniting in sheets of dark flame, and tore into the creature's chitin with devastating effect. Though the creature's regeneration ultimately overwhelmed their efforts, the Black Tide Wolf cavalry held the line when all else was failing.

A Wengari who rides a Black Fire Tide Wolf is considered among the elite. Bonding with such a creature requires a trial of fire—literally—as the prospective rider must endure the wolf's shadow flare without flinching.

## Rarity

Black Fire Tide Wolves are exceedingly rare. At any given time, no more than a handful are known to exist in the Rune Belt. Their population is not independently sustainable—the mutation does not reliably pass to offspring. Each one is a unique occurrence, a dark gift of Flicker's unpredictable influence.
