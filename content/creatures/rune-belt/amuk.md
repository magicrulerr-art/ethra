## Physical Description

Massive war-beasts resembling elephants with eight powerful legs. They stand enormous, their thick grey hides scarred from countless battles, their tusks worn from siege work. The eight legs give them extraordinary stability — they can push forward through broken terrain where normal beasts would stumble, and anchor themselves against the heaviest charges.

## Usage

Ten Amuk were deployed alongside scorpion riders and Tide Wolf cavalry during the Humman assault on Styxian, demonstrating their role as heavy shock troops. They serve as living siege engines — walking battering rams that break walls and formations alike.

## Relationship to Hummans

Part of the Humman military strategy relying on their numerical advantage. Hummans breed and train Amuk as heavy assault units, their slow but unstoppable advance a terror to face on the battlefield. The steady thunder of eight legs shaking the ground is a sound that breaks morale before the first blow lands.

## Combat Role

Heavy assault units capable of breaking enemy formations and holding strategic positions. Their eight-legged gait makes them nearly impossible to trip or flank, and their thick hides turn aside most conventional weapons.
