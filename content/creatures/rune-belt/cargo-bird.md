# Cargo Bird

**Classification:** Transport / Beast of Burden
**Biome:** Rune Belt
**Attunement:** None (mild, if any)
**Size:** Large — taller than a human
**Diet:** Omnivore — belt grains, berries, grubs

## Physical Description
Large green-plumed birds of the Rune Belt, shaggy and gentle, built for endurance rather than speed. They wear worn leather cargo saddles loaded with rolled blankets, satchels and message tubes.

## Behavior
Not mounts but transport beasts: they carry supplies and messages between the scattered settlements of the belt, their handlers walking beside them or pilots at their reins. Left at a gate, they wait with their pilots for as long as they are asked. They cannot laugh — and yet they make a sound exactly like it, usually at the worst possible moment.

## Diplomatic Record
After the Great War, cargo birds carried the humble delegation that crossed the desert to beg for peace, and waited at the gate of Ethrax while the new Sultan met Ajani (Arc VI, Chapter 2). Radys maintains, against all naturalists, that the cargo birds were laughing that day.

## Note
In the hands of a skilled rider, the old belt saying goes, a cargo bird "can become something else entirely." No one has needed to find out what that means — yet.
