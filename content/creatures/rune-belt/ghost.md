# Ghost

**Classification:** Aerial Mount  
**Biome:** Rune Belt  
**Attunement:** Flicker-attuned  
**Size:** Giant bat, 5-meter wingspan

## Physical Description

Giant bats native to the Rune Belt, with a five-meter wingspan that blots out the stars in flight. Their bodies are lean and aerodynamic, covered in dark fur, with wide membranous wings. Named "ghosts" for their ability to manipulate light and achieve **optical camouflage**, making them flicker in and out of visibility.

## Magical Properties

Flicker-attuned, the Ghosts can bend light around themselves to become nearly invisible — much like the Tide Wolves of the northern belt. Their optical camouflage pulses with the rhythm of drums used by their Motted Paw handlers. When tired, the camouflage flickers with exhaustion.

## Communication & Bonding

Ghosts communicate through **bone vibration** — a resonant frequency that travels through the hollow chambers of their bodies. The Motted Paws have learned this language over millennia and speak to them through **drums**. A drummer wears a drum harness strapped across their chest, beating out rhythms that the ghosts understand and respond to. Formations shift with each new rhythm.

## Role in Wengari Society

The Motted Paws rear ghosts in RiverSong, their city built atop fifteen-meter-tall jade trees in the heart of the Rune Belt. They use them to:
- Hunt in the basin
- Defend their cities from airborne threats
- Watch the shadows where the light of Steadfast cannot reach
- Transport elite warriors across long distances

## Combat Role

During the Great War, a hundred ghosts flew to the capital as reinforcements, led by General Yvaria of the Stripe Paws riding the largest ghost. Their optical camouflage made them devastating aerial assets — silent, fast, and nearly invisible until the moment of attack. Each ghost carries a rider with a drum harness and weapons.
