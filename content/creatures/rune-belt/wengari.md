# Wengari

**Classification:** Sentient Race / Anchor Race of Ethra  
**Biome:** Steadfast Desert (homeland) — families across Rune Belt and Umbral Ring  
**Attunement:** Steadfast-attuned; Aura, ember, and snow disciplines per family

## Physical Description

Feline humanoids — lithe, digitigrade, with coats as varied as the families that bear them: the sun-gold manes of the Bright Paws, the tiger-stripes of the Stripe Paws, the dusk-mottling of the Mottled Paws, the shadow-grey of the Shadow Paws, and the white fur of the Snow Paws. All share the same upright carriage, the same whiskered muzzles, the same eyes that catch the light of both suns.

## The Five Families

- **Bright Paws** — the royal family of the Steadfast Desert, seat at Styxian, keepers of the Pact and the hanging gardens of the Kyre Tree.
- **Shadow Paws** — scarred elders and long-memory keepers; Kareth among them.
- **Stripe Paws** — nomadic hunters of the Rune Belt's temperate forests, masters of Aura-hardened fur.
- **Mottled Paws** — dappled-coat stalkers of the Rune Belt's border woods.
- **Snow Paws** — the far-north family of the Umbral Ring, keepers of the Ice City.

## Magical Properties

Steadfast-attuned at the root, with each family refining its own discipline: Aura control (Stripe), shadow-reading (Shadow), ember-call (Bright), and the cold patience of snow (Snow). A White Dawn — a rare luminous-born heir — appears once in generations among the Bright Paws.

## Culture

A warrior culture bound by the Pact: three thousand years ago the Bright Paw royal line bargained with the Kyre Tree — prosperity for the Wengari in exchange for a worthy heir each generation. The Pact shapes succession, pilgrimage, and grief alike. Ajani Brightmane, the White Dawn, went to the Tree as the Convergence touched him, and returned bearing a new promise: the Road, a corridor from Flickermarch to the Steadfast Desert, fifty years in the building.

## Relationship to Ethra

The Wengari are the anchor race of the present age — desert-dwellers first, but their families range from the fungal glow of Flickermarch's edge to the frozen towers of the Umbral Ring. Where the Wengari walk, the other races of Ethra watch: the Humman empire in fear of another Tyrant, the Veylar in patient curiosity, the Threx in silence.
