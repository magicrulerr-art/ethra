## Physical Description

Giant lupine creatures, omnivores, the undisputed apex predators of the Rune Belt. Their fur shimmers with a subtle optical quality that hints at their magical adaptation.

## Magical Properties

Tide Wolves have evolved to digest the rare **Lotus Bloom**, generating specialized Aura effects:

- **Optical diffraction** — near-invisibility when moving through dappled light
- **Localized light bursts** — to blind or disorient prey and threats
- **Mirages** — complex optical illusions to confuse and disorient

## Behavior

They hunt in packs with coordinated precision. Extremely hard to kill due to their magical defenses. Killing a Tide Wolf is a **rite of passage** — a test not just of strength but of perception, courage, and the ability to see through magical deceit.

## Relationship to Wengari

Tide Wolves are considered worthy prey — a Wengari who can successfully hunt one proves their mastery of both physical combat and magical discernment. A clean blow from a Wengari can sever a Tide Wolf, showing the strength differential between the races.

## Lifecycle

Their population is tied to the availability of Lotus Bloom, creating a tight, high-stakes predator-prey dynamic. They are territorial and fiercely protective of their hunting grounds.
