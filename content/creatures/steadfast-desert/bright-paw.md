## Physical Description

A Wengari family resembling lion-like humanoid felines. Golden fur with distinctive mane-like hair around the head and shoulders.

## Magical Properties

Like all Wengari, masters of Aura control. The Bright Paw Royal Family possesses a unique legacy: they can harness Sorcery to conjure **hard-light constructs** into weapons of their choice, making them the undisputed martial masters of Ethra.

## Culture

Now more agricultural than other Wengari families. Respect might and power, frowning upon races that skew toward pure intellect or commerce. The ruling faction for over a thousand years.

## The Third Tyrant — Uthgar Lightbringer

Born at the beginning of a Tide cycle. A great administrator who built sprawling desert cities (now ruins). He advanced Sorcery for the Bright Paws, creating the royal inheritance that endures to this day. Vanished into the Chamber of Reflection.

## Relationship to Desert

The Bright Paws inhabit the harsh Steadfast Desert, drawing water from the Kyre Tree through root speaker networks. Their desert cities are ancient ruins now, monuments to their ancestor's ambition.
