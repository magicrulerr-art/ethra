# Styx

**Classification:** Flying Omen-Creature  
**Biome:** Steadfast Desert  
**Attunement:** Steadfast-attuned

## Physical Description

Flying creatures native to the Steadfast Desert. Births and migrations are read as omens by the Pyrinae. Feathers are a valued trade good.

## Cultural Significance

The Pyrinae read Styx births and migrations as prophecies. Their appearances mark significant celestial events and Tide cycles.

## Riding

Ridable by exceptional individuals. The Veylar Tyrant Xal'thyra was the first and only known to ride a Styx, breaking the white flame to her will.

## Trade

Styx feathers are highly valued across Ethra, though the creatures themselves rarely leave the desert skies.
