# Fire Beetle

**Classification:** Hostile Creature  
**Biome:** Steadfast Desert  
**Attunement:** Flicker-attuned

## Physical Description

Small but dangerous creatures with black chitin that absorbs heat. Visible only by the shimmer of air above them and the occasional glint of chitin.

## Behavior

Press against the desert borders, particularly active at dawn and dusk when Flicker's influence is strongest. Swarm in groups, overwhelming through numbers rather than individual strength.

## Combat Notes

Require garrison deployment to manage their incursions. Fire Beetles pose a constant low-level threat to desert settlements, emerging from cracks and crevices in the sandstone.
