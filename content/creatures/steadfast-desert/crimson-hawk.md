# Crimson Hawk

**Classification:** War-Bird / Aerial Bombardier
**Biome:** Steadfast Desert
**Attunement:** Steadfast
**Size:** Medium (hawk-sized)
**Diet:** Carnivore — small desert rodents and lizards

## Physical Description
Red plumage that burns against the pale morning sky; wings beating in the swift, steady rhythm of steadfast-tuned predators. In tight formation their aura adaptations hum with stored energy, visible as a faint heat-shimmer over the feathers.

## Abilities
- **Explosive feather volleys** — feathers detach from the body in volleys, each one a small, brilliant crimson detonation of light.
- **Steadfast aura storage** — the birds bank desert heat and light between volleys.
- **Thermal glide** — feathers spent, a crimson hawk is too exhausted to fly; it glides home on the thermals.

## War Record
Released in the first hours of the Great War (Arc V, Chapter 1): forty birds above the advancing scorpion-rider vanguard. Five volleys broke the charge and bought the capital's defenders time to regroup. Thirty-two of the forty returned; the defenders counted every one.

## Cultural Significance
The Pyrinae read a crimson hawk's return the way they read a Styx's flight — as an omen. A hawk that comes home featherless has spent itself for the city, and is fed from the hand for the rest of its days.
