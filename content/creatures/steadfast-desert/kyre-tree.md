## Physical Description

A massive, ancient desert organism that serves as both spiritual center and water source for the Pyrinae. Connected to the Mycelial Deep. One of the few entities that knows about "the thing below the belt."

## Role

- Spiritual center for Pyrinae cosmology
- Water source in the harsh desert climate
- Communication hub via root speaker networks
- Guardian of ancient knowledge, keeper of desert secrets

## Relationship to Convergence

One of the few entities that knows about "the thing below the belt" alongside the Veylar and the Mycelial Deep. Judges those who seek its petals but does not love.

## Cultural Significance

Pyrinae root speakers commune with the Kyre Tree through underground root networks. Its health directly affects desert settlements and the flow of water.

## Trade

Connected to rune-glass production — the Pyrinae create their famed Sorcerously imbued glass in proximity to the Tree's influence.
