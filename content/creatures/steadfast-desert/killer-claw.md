# Killer Claw (Suicide Raptor)

**Classification:** One-Way War-Beast
**Biome:** Steadfast Desert
**Attunement:** Steadfast (aura-enhanced speed)
**Size:** Medium raptor
**Diet:** Carnivore — desert hares and beetles (in peace)

## Physical Description
Sleek raptor bodies, low and streamlined, built to cross sand at hurricane speed. Kept in pens beneath the capital's western wall.

## Abilities
- **Aura-enhanced speed** — rune-amplified; they cross the killing ground in seconds.
- **Rune-bomb harness** — each beast is rigged with a package of compressed sorcerous destruction set to detonate on impact.

## War Record
Thirty were released in the opening hours of the Great War (Arc V, Chapter 1), from the pens beneath the western wall. They did not reach the vanguard to fight. They simply ran, and detonated at its heart. The glazed crater they left still hangs over the desert's memory. The killer claws that bought the capital three minutes were dead, every one.

## Cultural Significance
The capital's most solemn weapon. No handler gives the release-order twice; the pens are opened with a moment of silence, and the names of the thirty are read at the Remembrance of the Road.
