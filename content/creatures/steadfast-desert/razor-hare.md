# Razor Hare

**Classification:** Prey  
**Biome:** Steadfast Desert  
**Attunement:** Steadfast-attuned  
**Size:** Small (hare-sized)

## Physical Description

Small, swift hare-like creatures native to the Steadfast Desert, covered in razor-sharp plates that protrude from their fur like a hedgehog's spines — hard, sharp keratin plates that make them impossible to eat. Their bodies are lean and compact, built for short bursts of speed across the open sand. Their most distinctive trait is their eyes, which are attuned to the Kyre Tree's aura and can see the otherwise invisible **kyre flowers**.

## Behavior

Razor hares are cautious, skittish prey. They gather at water holes at dawn and dusk, their most vulnerable moments. They are constantly alert for the shadow of a Styx overhead — the death from above that is their primary predator.

## Unique Ability

Only razor hares can see and harvest kyre flowers — the pale, invisible blossoms of the Kyre Tree that grow along the city walls and at the desert wells. The Pyrinae keep razor hares in their menageries for this purpose, using them to gather the flowers when needed for rituals or healing.

## Relationship to the Desert Ecosystem

The razor hare sits at the bottom of the desert food chain, the primary prey of the Styx. They are a symbol of knowing when to flee and when to fight — as the old saying goes: "There is strength in knowing when to flee and when to fight, like the razor hare knows." Despite their lowly position, they play a crucial role in the desert's magical ecology as the only creatures that can interact with the Kyre Tree's distributed flowers.

## In Wengari Culture

The razor hare is a common metaphor in Wengari speech — used to describe prey that is aware of its place in the natural order, or situations where discretion is the better part of valor. "Looking at it the way a razor hare looks at a Styx" means facing something overwhelmingly dangerous with grim acceptance.
