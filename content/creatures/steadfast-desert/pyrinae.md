# Pyrinae

**Classification:** Sentient Race / Anchor Species  
**Biome:** Steadfast Desert  
**Attunement:** Steadfast-attuned

## Physical Description

Shorter than Wengari, reptilian-descended humanoids with ochre/copper scales and heat-sensing pits — vestigial remnants of their reptilian ancestors. Their short reptilian features and deliberate, graceful movements mark them as a patient, ancient people. Adapted for survival in the harsh desert climate.

## Magical Properties

Steadfast-attuned Sorcery expressed through water-heat-mineral manipulation — a survival adaptation in the harsh desert. Their abilities allow them to locate and draw water, shape stone, and work minerals.

## Culture

Astronomers and survivalists with a deeply spiritual relationship with the **Kyre Tree**. Styx birth omens shape their worldview. They practice root speaking through underground networks connected to the Kyre Tree.

## Trade

Renowned for their **rune-glass** — glass imbued with Sorcerous properties — and **Styx feathers**, both highly valued trade goods. Have a caravan culture known as "the fire feet."

## Relationship with Wengari

Historical allies with the Bright Paws. After the Third Tyrant's era, the Pyrinae were vassals of the Wengari; later elevated to friends and peers. They have fought alongside the Wengari in multiple conflicts.
