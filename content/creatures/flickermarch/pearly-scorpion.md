**Classification:** Mount/Creature  
**Biome:** Flickermarch  
**Attunement:** Flicker-attuned

## Physical Description

Large scorpion-like creatures with pearlescent exoskeletons that catch and reflect light in shimmering patterns. Adapted to marsh environments with specialized water-sensing antennae. Approximately five meters in length — large enough to serve as riding mounts for Humman cavalry.

## Domestication

Traded primarily by the Hummans. Wild scorpions are located and overwhelmed through sheer numbers, then domesticated as mounts and egg-layers. The Humman strategy relies on their numerical advantage to subdue these creatures.

## Usage

- **Mounts** for Humman cavalry units operating in marsh terrain
- **Egg-layers** for breeding programs
- **Trade goods** exported across Ethra

## Combat Notes

Ten Amuk were deployed alongside scorpion riders and Tide Wolf cavalry during the Humman assault on Styxian, showing their effectiveness in coordinated warfare across varied terrain.
