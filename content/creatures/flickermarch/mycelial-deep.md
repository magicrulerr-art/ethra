# Mycelial Deep

<img src="/ethra/static/images/mycelial-deep.png" alt="Mycelial Deep — the living fungal network beneath the Flickermarch" class="creature-portrait" style="max-width:400px;float:right;margin:0 0 1em 1em;">

**Classification:** Subterranean Network / Living Entity  
**Biome:** Flickermarch (origin), spans beneath all biomes  
**Attunement:** Flicker-attuned  

## Physical Description

A global mycelial network that pulses beneath Ethra's surface. In the Flickermarch, it manifests as the Mycelial Deep — a living fungal organism that serves as parent and guide to the Threx.

## Connection

The Mycelial Deep is connected to the Kyre Tree, sharing knowledge across vast distances. It preserves collective memory for the Threx through spore-based consciousness transfer.

## Relationship to Threx

The Threx view the fungal network as their parent and guide. Children of the Deep, they communicate through mycelial hum and Sporefall dreams. The Lament forms are direct avatars of this network.

## Powers

- Collective memory preservation through mycelial threads
- Sporefall dreams that connect all Threx across vast distances
- Manipulation of marsh terrain and fungal growth
- Sensing disturbances through root-network perception

## Ancient Knowledge

One of the few entities that knows about "the thing below the belt" alongside the Veylar and Kyre Tree. Contains memories stretching back to the earliest Convergences.
