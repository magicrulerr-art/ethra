## Physical Description

The apex predator of the Flickermarch — a massive serpentine creature reaching fifteen meters in length, covered in iridescent scales that shimmer in the marsh light. Known as the **Iris Scale** or simply the **"green death"** by those who have survived an encounter. They are faster than a Styx's dive and deadlier than a Tide Wolf's jaws. Their bodies are long, sinuous, and built for explosive speed — lunging from coiled positions with terrifying acceleration.

## Behavior

Ambush predators that rely on speed and venom to overwhelm prey. They coil in the marsh shadows, their iridescent scales blending with the toxic mists of the Flickermarch, and strike with a speed that defies their size. Extreme aggression — they attack anything that enters their territory.

## Relationship to the Threx

The Threx, particularly the Lament L'vat, use Iris Serpents as training tools. Ajani was dropped into a pit with them for a year — the experience taught him the saber form he would later use to win his crown. The serpents are respected but not domesticated; they are the wild heart of the March.

## Venom

Highly venomous. A single bite can kill even a Wengari. The venom is fast-acting and excruciating, designed to immobilize prey instantly.

## Combat Notes

Their speed makes them nearly impossible to fight with slow weapons. The saber is the only weapon fast enough to match their lunges, long enough to keep their fangs at bay, and responsive enough to exploit the brief openings their attacks create. The spear recovers too slowly. The ji is too heavy. The sword lacks the saber's vicious slashing speed.
