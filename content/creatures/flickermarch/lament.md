## Physical Description

Rare avatars of the Mycelial Deep itself, ancient and patient. Their bodies radiate the quiet, inexorable presence of the Deep. Extremely powerful and imposing.

## Behavior

Ancient and patient beings who serve as the voice of the Mycelial Deep. Their multifaceted eyes perceive across multiple spectrums, reading both light and the flow of mycelial threads.

## Notable Individuals

L'vat is a known Lament who trained Ajani in the ways of the Mycelial Deep before his coronation.

## Powers

Extremely powerful in mycelial manipulation. Can shape the marsh itself, command Sporefall dreams, and are said to be able to halt invading armies through sheer presence alone.

## Role in Threx Society

Each Lament carries the direct will of the Deep. They are the voice that speaks to outsiders and the hand that guides their race through Convergences.
