## Physical Description

Mature Threx who have rooted themselves into the mycelial network. Patient and wise guardians of the Deep.

## Behavior

Serve as wise caretakers of the Mycelial Deep. Communicate through mycelial hum and Sporefall dreams. Connected to the global fungal network that preserves collective memory.

## Role

The backbone of Threx society. They maintain the network, guide the Quick, and interpret the will of the Deep.

## Special Abilities

Can draw upon the Mycelial Deep's collective knowledge and sense disturbances across vast distances. Their chitin becomes tougher and more resistant as they root deeply into the network.
