# Quick Threx

**Classification:** Young Threx Form  
**Biome:** Flickermarch  
**Attunement:** Flicker-attuned

## Physical Description

Young Threx, impulsive and curious. Barely chest-height on a Wengari (approximately three to four feet tall). Bodies covered in iridescent chitin that shifts through prismatic colors in sunlight.

## Behavior

Rarely survive more than a week away from the Mycelial Deep. Communicate through mycelial hum and Sporefall dreams.

## Lifecycle

Immature Threx who have not yet rooted into the network. They are the scouts and explorers, gathering information for the Deep.

## Vulnerability

Without connection to the Mycelial Deep, they weaken rapidly. Their short lifespan makes them fearless but reckless.
