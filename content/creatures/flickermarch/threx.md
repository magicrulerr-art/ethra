# Threx

**Classification:** Sentient Race / Mycelial Collective  
**Biome:** Flickermarch  
**Attunement:** Deep-attuned; mycelial communion beneath the fungal forest  

<picture><img src="/static/images/lament-v2.png" alt="The Lament, a Threx" class="creature-portrait" style="max-width:400px;float:right;margin:0 0 1em 1em;border:2px solid #333;"></picture>

## Physical Description

Mycelial beings of the Flickermarch, fruiting bodies of a vast underground network the surface races call the Deep. A Threx body is a woven column of fungal flesh, crowned with caps and pores, its many-faceted eyes catching Flicker's crimson pulse like wet stone.

## The Three Forms

- **The Lament** — the contemplative form; silent, multifaceted, the shape in which the Threx meet the surface world. The Lament that walked beside Ajani Brightmane spoke without sound and grieved without tears.
- **The Quick** — the mobile hunters and errand-runners of the fungal forest, swift and light-boned.
- **The Rooted** — the anchored nodes, half-buried elders through whom the Deep thinks slowly across centuries.

## Magical Properties

Every Threx is a node in the Deep, the mycelial network beneath Ethra. Thought travels the threads; memory is shared, sedimented, and re-consumed. What the Deep knows, the Rooted remember; what the Rooted remember, the Lament carry to the surface.

## Culture

The Threx do not build cities; they fruit where the Deep surfaces. Their politics are chemical, their history stratified like soil. They stir only when the world stirs — and as the Convergence approaches, the Deep has begun to hum.

## Relationship to Ethra

Ancient neutrals. The Threx warred with no one and traded with everyone, until the Blight — the Chi'Thak swarm that consumes all — reminded the surface races that the Deep is also a shield. The Lament's covenant with Ajani Brightmane, the Road's promise, and the quiet watch kept on the one in the Rune-Belt all bind the Threx now to the surface world's fate.
