- **Race:** Wengari (Mottled Paws)
- **Faith:** Testing Fire
- **Region:** Rune Belt Basin

Mottled Paw city in the heart of the Rune Belt basin, built atop the jade trees — fifteen-meter refulgent hardwoods, hard as rock and shining, raised against the optic camouflage of the Belt's predators. Keepers of the ghosts: five-meter camouflaged bats, spoken to through drums. Here since before the Pact.
