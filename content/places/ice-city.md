The great city of ice — the Snow Paws' frozen citadel in the far north of the Umbral Ring, carved from ancient ice and stone, where the family gathers in the harshest winters and in times of war. From it the Peregrines launch on Sunraptor-back and Wurm-glides cross the tundra. Seat of Cefiro, Prince of the Snow Paws.
