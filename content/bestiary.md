## SENTIENT RACES

Seven sentient races survive on Ethra, remnants of a once more diverse population. 80 million years of planetary history, 20,000 years of sentient life. The Crimson Tide has winnowed them down to these seven survivors.

**See also:** [`RACES.md`](RACES.md) — the master races index page with reading order, biome, and attunement summary across all seven. The following section preserves the original long-form entries that previously lived here as the master page.

### 1. THE WENGARI

**Designation:** Anchor Species of the Rune Belt (and Bright Paw branch in the Steadfast Desert)

**Physical Description:** Tall anthropomorphic felines. Five surviving families, each mimicking a distinct Earth big cat:

| Family | Feline Type | Habitat |
|--------|-------------|---------|
| Shadow Paws | Panthers | Rune Belt |
| Mottled Paws | Jaguars | Rune Belt |
| Bright Paws | Lions | Steadfast Desert |
| Stripe Paws | Tigers | Rune Belt |
| Snow Paws | Snow Leopards | Umbral Ring |

<picture><source srcset="/static/images/bright-paw-v2.webp" type="image/webp" /><source srcset="/static/images/bright-paw-v2.jpg" type="image/jpeg" /><img src="/static/images/bright-paw-v2.png" alt="Bright Paw Warrior" class="creature-portrait" style="max-width:350px;float:right;margin:0 0 1em 1em;border:2px solid #333;"></picture>

**Magical Attunement:** Steadfast-attuned, yet they are **masters of Aura control** — a rare dual-nature born from constant Tide pressure. They use Aura to harden their fur into living armor and enhance claws and strikes to deliver devastating blows. A clean blow from a Wengari can sever a Tide Wolf. They do not carry weapons; their bodies are their armaments.

The **Bright Paw Royal Family** possesses a unique legacy: they can harness Sorcery to conjure **hard-light constructs** into weapons of their choice, making them the undisputed martial masters of Ethra. Other races call them the **"White Death."** Despite the techniques being public, no other family can replicate this — it is suspected to be bloodline-linked.

**Culture:** Deeply xenophobic, warlike, belligerent, meritocratic. Previously nomadic and warlike, now more agricultural for the Bright Paws who live in the desert; the rest remain deeply nomadic. They respect might and power, frowning upon races that skew toward pure intellect or commerce, viewing them as cowards or weak.

**Internal Politics:** The Bright Paws have been the ruling faction for over a thousand years and four Tide cycles due to their overwhelming might. No other family has been able to match them.

**The Tyrants of the Wengari:** Two tyrants emerged from this race:
- **The First Tyrant** — A Shadow Paw who unified most of the 20 families of his time. He lived 300 years. For the first 150, an excellent leader. As the second Tide of his lifetime approached, he grew increasingly paranoid, mandating the genocide of entire families and often delivering the end personally. The Shadow Paws are deeply ashamed of him; it remains a source of tension with other families.
- **The Third Tyrant — Uthgar Lightbringer** — Born at the beginning of a Tide cycle. A great administrator who built sprawling desert cities (now ruins). He advanced Sorcery for the Bright Paws, creating the royal inheritance that endures to this day. He defies the title of "tyrant" — his legacy is more complex.

### 2. THE PYRINAE

<picture><source srcset="/static/images/pyrinae-v2.webp" type="image/webp" /><source srcset="/static/images/pyrinae-v2.jpg" type="image/jpeg" /><img src="/static/images/pyrinae-v2.png" alt="Pyrinae Astronomer" class="creature-portrait" style="max-width:350px;float:right;margin:0 0 1em 1em;border:2px solid #333;"></picture>

**Designation:** Anchor Species of the Steadfast Desert

**Physical Description:** Reptilian-descended humanoid with ochre/copper scales, heat-sensing pits, shorter than Wengari. Steadfast-attuned.

**Magical Attunement:** Steadfast-attuned. Their Sorcery is expressed primarily through water-heat-mineral manipulation — a survival adaptation in the harsh desert. Their Sorcery allows them to locate and draw water, shape stone, and work minerals.

**Culture:** Astronomers, survivalists. Deeply spiritual relationship with the **Kyre Tree**, a massive, ancient desert organism that serves as both spiritual center and water source. **Styx** birth omens shape their worldview — the Styx are flying creatures whose births and migrations are read as prophecies.

**Trade:** The Pyrinae are renowned for their **rune-glass** — glass imbued with Sorcerous properties — and **Styx feathers**, both highly valued trade goods.

**Relationship with Wengari:** Historical allies (specifically with the Bright Paws). After the Third Tyrant's era, the Pyrinae were vassals of the Wengari; later elevated to friends and peers. They have fought alongside the Wengari in multiple conflicts.

**Notable Features:**
- Root speakers — a form of communication through underground root networks connected to the Kyre Tree
- Caravan culture ("the fire feet") — extensive desert trade routes
- Rehabilitation of desert cities — skilled builders and restorers of the Third Tyrant's ruins

### 3. THE DRAGARI

<picture><source srcset="/static/images/dragari-v5.webp" type="image/webp" /><source srcset="/static/images/dragari-v5.jpg" type="image/jpeg" /><img src="/static/images/dragari-v5.png" alt="Dragari sea-farer" class="creature-portrait" style="max-width:350px;float:right;margin:0 0 1em 1em;border:2px solid #333;"></picture>

**Designation:** Anchor Species of the Umbral Ring

**Physical Description:** Three-meter-tall lizard-like beings with powerful arms and **iridescent scales**. Two geographic variants:
- **North-dwelling:** Deep green hue
- **South-dwelling:** Deep blue hue

**Magical Attunement:** Flicker-attuned, but **forcefully adapted to Steadfast** — a biological paradox. Their Aura control allows them to harness oxygen from water via skin osmosis, enabling exceptional underwater capabilities.

**Culture:** The Dragari are the quiet heart of the Umbral Ring — a race defined not by conquest or knowledge but by profound, ancient gentleness. They are one of the **most ancient races on Ethra**.

- Deeply spiritual: they believe everything has a soul and every living being deserves respect
- Fearful of other races (not xenophobic — fearful); seldom let themselves be seen
- Deeply familial society, living in multi-generational homes
- History is oral, handed down via songs — much is lost to time
- They meet once every five years in a rotating schedule
- Isolationist by nature and ideology

**Combat & Survival:**
- Weak on land, but **formidable in water** — they can use Aura to propel themselves at sub-sonic speeds, creating waves to stun and harvest fish
- Most legends of sea monsters and glowing serpents are their doing
- They survive the Tides by communing with an **Auruch** (giant sea creature), offering fish in exchange for protection

**Symbiotic Relationships:**
- **Auruchs** — Revered as "long-lost brothers." The Dragari commune with them via songs and offerings, and protect them from raiding parties.
- **Sea Marsh** — Each family cultivates a small amount. The High Shaman is chosen by the marsh. It is anathema to let it die or suffer. This is how they manage Auruch communion.

**Life Span:** Rarely surpass 50 years.

### 4. THE THREX

**Designation:** Anchor Species of the Flickermarch

**Physical Description:** Fungal-insectoid beings, barely chest-height on a Wengari (approximately three to four feet tall). Their bodies are covered in iridescent chitin that shifts through prismatic colors when caught in sunlight. They possess multifaceted eyes that provide them with compound vision across multiple spectrums. Three distinct metamorphic forms exist:
- **Quick:** Young Threx, impulsive and curious, rarely surviving more than a week away from the Mycelial Deep
- **Rooted:** Mature Threx, patient and wise, who have rooted themselves in the mycelial network
- **Lament:** Rare avatars of the Deep itself, ancient and patient, their bodies radiating the quiet, inexorable presence of the Mycelial Deep

**Magical Attunement:** Flicker-attuned. Connected to the Mycelial Deep through a global mycelial network that preserves their collective memory. They communicate through mycelial hum and Sporefall dreams.

**Culture:** Children of the Mycelial Deep, the Threx view the fungal network as their parent and guide. They were scarred by the First Tyrant's Black Fire and deeply fear the Wengari, remembering both the Black Fire burnings and the Fifth Tyrant's Plague. Despite this history, Ajani has earned their trust through his training under L'vat, a Lament avatar, and his promise of the Road.

**External Relations:** The Threx have historically been on poor terms with Hummans — they remember being burned by Plague weapons twice before. They were initially hostile to Humman refugees after the war, but Ajani's intervention and the promise of trade (baubles, clean water, medicinal herbs) has begun to thaw relations.

**Notable Features:**
- Three metamorphic stages: Quick, Rooted, and Lament
- Connected through a global mycelial network that preserves collective memory
- Fear of extinction when away from the March network
- Unique bioluminescent capabilities through mycelial connection

### 5. HUMMANS

**Designation:** Staple Species of the Rune Belt (with settlements across multiple biomes)

**Physical Description:** By all intents and purposes, normal Earth-like humans.

**Magical Attunement:** Flicker-attuned, but hold a **negligible amount of Aura**. They are not particularly attuned to Sorcery either.

**The Youngest Race:** Evolved during a Tide a mere **thousand years ago**. They are by far the weakest and youngest of the races in Ethra.

**Evolutionary Boon:** While other races have long gestation periods and are confined to single biomes, Hummans have:
- Large offspring counts
- Low gestation periods
- Low Aura requirements (require far less sustenance than other races)
- Numbers that have grown to **eclipse all other races combined**

**Culture:** Excellent merchants and politicians. They have forged alliances with every sentient race except the Threx. Their capital is **Verdantis** in the Rune Belt. They have settlements across the Steadfast Desert and have begun carefully expanding into the Flickermarch and the Umbral Ring.

**Trade:** Their primary export is **Pearly Scorpion mounts and eggs**. Their numbers make it easy to locate and overwhelm the wild scorpions, which they then export across Ethra.

**The Fifth Tyrant — Velarius Vane:** A Humman tyrant whose legacy is more recent than any other. His horror was a **plague** — a slow-spreading, impossible-to-defend poison that lasted 65 years, mutating and evolving, killing everything it touched. Immunity was impossible and death was excruciating. Unlike the older races who have had millennia to process their tyrants' legacies, the Hummans still live in the shadow of theirs. This is fresh history.

### 6. THE CHI'THAK

**Designation:** Anchor Species of the Underground (Sixth Biome)

**Physical Description:** Small insect-like beings, about the size of a small cat. They possess:
- **Six appendages:** Two used for walking on land, all six for "earth swimming"
- **A pair of wings** for flying

**Magical Attunement:** Steadfast-attuned **and** Flicker-capable — a rare dual-attunement.

**Culture:** The proverbial "blight upon the land." No one knows where they came from or how old they are, but **everyone knows when they are coming.**

- They emerge only during **Convergences** (the Resonance), from underground burrows
- Their flight **blots out the sky**
- They **devour everything in sight** — everything. Large swaths of land have been laid barren
- To other races, they appear unintelligent and incapable of communication
- In reality, they are **extremely sophisticated**, possessing an **underground network that spans the entire globe**, including the deep oceans

**Communication:** Via **pheromones**. Hive mentality.

**Castes:**
- **Warriors** — Slightly larger and more formidable
- **Workers** — Smaller, white, never go outside
- **Harvesters** — The only caste other races encounter
- **Queen** — Extremely long-lived

**Survival Cycle:** They cannot sustain their own race. On every Convergence, they must go out and feed. Scouts that return engorged are **consumed by the colony** to endure until the next cycle. No Chi'Thak ever survives two cycles — their drive is cannibalistic.

**The Second Tyrant — Crr'zzak, "The Fear on Wings":** A Chi'Thak who lived for only one Convergence — by far the youngest of the tyrants ever to die. But that was enough. He was taller and stronger than every other member of his race due to Flicker's influence. He matured in a matter of days, just in time for the Convergence. When he emerged, the blight came with him. His pheromones were unusually potent — what was once a free-for-all became an organized, all-consuming plague. He flew from end to end of the continent in weeks, devouring everything, entire cities lost in seconds. When he reached the other end, he returned home and in his dying moments let himself be devoured by the colony along with all the nutrients and rich Aura he had amassed. Every current Chi'Thak owes its life to him, and he spurred an evolution in their species.

**Cities:** Vivid glowing stone murals depicting each "super-organism" (Tyrant-class beings) slaughtering them by the millions. They stay clear of these super-organisms.

### THE VEYLAR

**Designation:** Anchor Species of the Tidepools

**Physical Description:** Aquatic/semi-aquatic beings with iridescent skin that shifts through the colors of the tidepools (deep indigo, sea-green, pearl-white). Fin-ridges along their temples allow them to taste the air and sense chemical changes. Webbed hands and feet facilitate swimming. The Petal-Shell cradled in their hands pulses with soft, rhythmic light and serves as their primary tool for communication and magic.

**Magical Attunement:** Steadfast-attuned. Their magic flows through the Abyssal Heart (deep oceanic entity) and the Sea Marsh, allowing them to commune with the ocean's deepest trenches. They use the Resonant Network through Shell-Singers who can channel songs across entire oceans.

**Culture:** Extremely long-lived aquatic race with a fundamentally different sense of time. They are isolationist but maintain diplomatic relations. The Harmonic Council governs through deliberation and moon counsel. Shell-Singers serve as their primary diplomats and magic-users. The Veylar have never walked the land; they have no roads, no caravans, no cities beyond the Tidepools.

**Notable Features:**
- Wardens of the tides and the ocean depths
- Possess knowledge of "the thing that dwells within the belt" (shared only with the Kyre Tree, the Mycelial Deep, and Ajani)
- Communication through Petal-Shells that allow remote listening/transmission
- Have never been bested by the Wengari in combat — a point of immense pride
- Patient deliberation-based decision making; must sing old songs and consult the moon before acting
- The Veylar Queen holds immense authority and has never traveled to the surface (until recently)
- Only Tyrant to ever ride a Styx and break the white flame to her will

**The Veylar Tyrant — Xal'thyra:** Silenced the male Styx and projected the Great Stillness across the world. The only Tyrant to ride a Styx. Only Veylar to ever break the white flame to her will.

### THE STEADFAST DESERT

**Climate/Geography:** Arid desert. Home to the Bright Paw Wengari and Pyrinae.

**Apex Pair — The Styx and the Kyre Tree:**

### THE UMBRAL RING

**Climate/Geography:** Coastal/twilight zone. Home to the Dragari.

**Apex Creature — Auruch:**
Giant sea creatures revered as "long-lost brothers" by the Dragari.<span class="creature-inline-img"><picture><source srcset="/static/images/auruch.webp" type="image/webp" /><source srcset="/static/images/auruch.jpg" type="image/jpeg" /><img src="/static/images/auruch.png" alt="Auruch" class="creature-portrait" style="max-width:200px;float:right;margin:0 0 0.5em 0.5em;border:1px solid #555;"></picture></span> They are communed with via songs and offerings. The Dragari protect them from raiding parties from other races. During the Crimson Tide, Dragari survival depends on securing an Auruch's protection through offerings of fish.

**Flora — Sea Marsh:**
Cultivated by each Dragari family. The High Shaman is chosen by the marsh.<span class="creature-inline-img"><picture><source srcset="/static/images/sea-marsh.webp" type="image/webp" /><source srcset="/static/images/sea-marsh.jpg" type="image/jpeg" /><img src="/static/images/sea-marsh.png" alt="Sea Marsh" class="creature-portrait" style="max-width:200px;float:right;margin:0 0 0.5em 0.5em;border:1px solid #555;"></picture></span> Used to facilitate Auruch communion. Bioluminescent, responds to emotional states of the caretaker.

**Aerial Mount — Sunraptor:**
A peregrine variant native to the Ice City and northern territories of the Umbral Ring.<span class="creature-inline-img"><picture><source srcset="/static/images/sunraptor.webp" type="image/webp" /><source srcset="/static/images/sunraptor.jpg" type="image/jpeg" /><img src="/static/images/sunraptor.png" alt="Sunraptor" class="creature-portrait" style="max-width:200px;float:right;margin:0 0 0.5em 0.5em;border:1px solid #555;"></picture></span> Sunraptors are Flicker-attuned aerial predators that hunt by diving from great heights with the sun at their backs. They are fast — faster than anything in the sky except perhaps the Styx. The Snow Paws have tamed them as flying mounts for centuries, bonding with individual birds from hatching. They are not endurance flyers and require frequent rest, but their loyalty to their riders is absolute. Snow Paw Peregrine Cefiro famously flew a Sunraptor from the Ice City across the Steadfast Desert to reach Styxian.

**Mount — Snow Wurm:**
A massive Flicker-attuned arthropod used by the Snow Paws as their primary ground transport.<span class="creature-inline-img"><picture><source srcset="/static/images/snow-wurm.webp" type="image/webp" /><source srcset="/static/images/snow-wurm.jpg" type="image/jpeg" /><img src="/static/images/snow-wurm.png" alt="Snow Wurm" class="creature-portrait" style="max-width:200px;float:right;margin:0 0 0.5em 0.5em;border:1px solid #555;"></picture></span> Snow Wurms freeze the ground beneath them with their aura, then glide over the ice they create at startling speed. With hundreds of legs and a serpentine body, a single Wurm can traverse terrain impassable to wheeled carts. Two fully grown Snow Wurms can carry forty Wengari warriors with full provisions. The Snow Paws have bred them for centuries; Nikolai, a legendary Wurm-keeper, developed a rare royal line with golden chitin that lives twice as long as common Wurms.

### THE FLICKERMARCH

**Climate/Geography:** Marshland. Home to the Threx. Treacherous terrain of mud, mist, and ancient trees with no clear paths. The Mycelial Deep pulses beneath the surface. The landscape bears scars from the First Tyrant's Black Fire — blackened glass stretches where the fire burned.

**Creatures:**
- **Quick Threx:** <span class="creature-inline-img"><picture><source srcset="/static/images/quick-threx-v3.webp" type="image/webp" /><source srcset="/static/images/quick-threx-v3.jpg" type="image/jpeg" /><img src="/static/images/quick-threx-v3.png" alt="Quick Threx" style="max-width:160px;float:right;margin:0 0 0.5em 0.5em;border:1px solid #555;"></picture></span> Young, impulsive, barely chest-height on Wengari. Die within weeks of leaving the March. Communicate through mycelial hum and Sporefall dreams. Rarely seen outside the Flickermarch — their iridescent chitin and multifaceted eyes mark them as unique.
- **Rooted Threx:** <span class="creature-inline-img"><picture><source srcset="/static/images/rooted-threx-v2.webp" type="image/webp" /><source srcset="/static/images/rooted-threx-v2.jpg" type="image/jpeg" /><img src="/static/images/rooted-threx-v2.png" alt="Rooted Threx" style="max-width:160px;float:right;margin:0 0 0.5em 0.5em;border:1px solid #555;"></picture></span> Mature, patient, rooted into the mycelial network. Serve as wise caretakers of the Deep.
- **Lament Threx:** <span class="creature-inline-img"><picture><source srcset="/static/images/lament-v2.webp" type="image/webp" /><source srcset="/static/images/lament-v2.jpg" type="image/jpeg" /><img src="/static/images/lament-v2.png" alt="Lament Threx" style="max-width:160px;float:right;margin:0 0 0.5em 0.5em;border:1px solid #555;"></picture></span> Rare avatars of the Mycelial Deep itself, ancient and patient. - **Iris Serpent (Iris Scale):** <span class="creature-inline-img"><picture><source srcset="/static/images/iris-serpent.webp" type="image/webp" /><source srcset="/static/images/iris-serpent.jpg" type="image/jpeg" /><img src="/static/images/iris-serpent.png" alt="Iris Serpent" style="max-width:160px;float:right;margin:0 0 0.5em 0.5em;border:1px solid #555;"></picture></span> The "green death" — apex predator of the Flickermarch. Fifteen meters of iridescent serpent with venom and speed faster than a Styx. Used by L'vat to train Ajani.
- **Pearly Scorpion:** <span class="creature-inline-img"><picture><source srcset="/static/images/pearly-scorpion-v3.webp" type="image/webp" /><source srcset="/static/images/pearly-scorpion-v3.jpg" type="image/jpeg" /><img src="/static/images/pearly-scorpion-v3.png" alt="Pearly Scorpion" style="max-width:160px;float:right;margin:0 0 0.5em 0.5em;border:1px solid #555;"></picture></span> Large scorpion-like mounts, approximately five meters long, with pearlescent exoskeletons that shimmer in marsh light. Native to the Flickermarch. Traded and domesticated by the Hummans as cavalry mounts and egg-layers. Flicker-attuned.
- **Mycelial Deep:** <span class="creature-inline-img"><picture><source srcset="/static/images/mycelial-deep.webp" type="image/webp" /><source srcset="/static/images/mycelial-deep.jpg" type="image/jpeg" /><img src="/static/images/mycelial-deep.png" alt="Mycelial Deep" style="max-width:160px;float:right;margin:0 0 0.5em 0.5em;border:1px solid #555;"></picture></span> The vast living fungal network that pulses beneath the Flickermarch. Parent and guide to the Threx, connected to the Kyre Tree. Preserves collective memory through spore-based consciousness transfer. One of the few entities that knows of "the thing below the belt."

**Notable Features:**
- No clear landmarks or paths; navigation requires guidance from the Deep
- Dangerous to non-Threx due to mycelial network effects
- Deep scars from Black Fire and Plague weapons; Threx remember both burns

### THE UNDERGROUND (Sixth Biome)

**Climate/Geography:** Subterranean networks spanning the entire globe, including beneath oceans. Home to the Chi'Thak.

**Creatures:**
- Chi'Thak themselves are the dominant species
- Super-organisms (Tyrant-class beings) that the Chi'Thak avoid at all costs, depicted in glowing stone murals
- Thunder wasps — Flicker-attuned parasitoid swarms of the deep desert hives. In the Steadfast cycle they are harmless — their sting cannot pierce the thick chitin of most desert dwellers; in the Flicker cycle their aura grants them speed and hardness. They breed by stinging great beasts, dozens of larvae to a corpse, and almost never surface outside the tide.
- Sand wurms — legendary wurms of the deep desert, some twenty meters long, their gold-green chitin said to turn aside runed weapons. No one has seen one; their existence is only inferred by the corpses of lesser beasts left behind, and caves filled to the brim with bones and old husks.

## CONVERGENCE-TOUCHED

The **Convergence-Touched** are an extremely rare phenomenon. The only known living Convergence-Touched individual is **Ajani Brightmane**, heir to the Wengari throne — and even he did not know until his coronation ceremony revealed it.

When the Convergence touches a being, their runes (if Wengari) ignite with Convergence fire — a phenomenon not seen in living memory. The implications of this touch are unknown, even to the one who bears it.
