# Geography: The Six Biomes

*The supercontinent of Ethra — a world of stark contrasts and ancient landscapes.*

---

## The Supercontinent

Ethra's single supercontinent stretches from the northern polar region to the southern temperate zone, surrounded by a deep, mineral-rich world-ocean.

---

## The Six Biomes

### 1. The Rune Belt

<picture><source srcset="/static/images/biome-rune-belt.webp" type="image/webp" /><source srcset="/static/images/biome-rune-belt.jpg" type="image/jpeg" /><img src="/static/images/biome-rune-belt.png" alt="The Rune Belt landscape" class="creature-portrait" style="max-width:100%;width:100%;height:auto;border-radius:12px;margin-bottom:20px;border:2px solid #333;box-shadow:0 4px 20px rgba(0,0,0,0.3);"></picture>

- **Type:** Temperate, diverse ecosystems — the heartland
- **Inhabitants:** Wengari (Shadow Paws, Motted Paws, Stripe Paws), Hummans (capital: Verdantis)
- **Apex Predator:** Tide Wolves
- **Key Flora:** Lotus Blooms
- **Features:** The most politically significant biome. Site of the Humman capital (Verdantis). Rich in history and conflict.

### 2. The Steadfast Desert

<picture><source srcset="/static/images/biome-steadfast-desert.webp" type="image/webp" /><source srcset="/static/images/biome-steadfast-desert.jpg" type="image/jpeg" /><img src="/static/images/biome-steadfast-desert.png" alt="The Steadfast Desert landscape" class="creature-portrait" style="max-width:100%;width:100%;height:auto;border-radius:12px;margin-bottom:20px;border:2px solid #333;box-shadow:0 4px 20px rgba(0,0,0,0.3);"></picture>

- **Type:** Arid, harsh desert
- **Inhabitants:** Bright Paw Wengari, Pyrinae
- **Apex Life Form:** Kyre Tree
- **Key Creature:** Styx (flying creatures, omens, ridable)
- **Features:** Ruins of the Third Tyrant's cities. Pyrinae settlements built around Kyre Trees. Site of the Wengari capital (Styxian), built around the Kyre Tree's water and the hanging gardens. Rune-glass production.

### 3. The Umbral Ring

<picture><source srcset="/static/images/biome-umbral-ring.webp" type="image/webp" /><source srcset="/static/images/biome-umbral-ring.jpg" type="image/jpeg" /><img src="/static/images/biome-umbral-ring.png" alt="The Umbral Ring landscape" class="creature-portrait" style="max-width:100%;width:100%;height:auto;border-radius:12px;margin-bottom:20px;border:2px solid #333;box-shadow:0 4px 20px rgba(0,0,0,0.3);"></picture>

- **Type:** Coastal, twilight-lit zone
- **Inhabitants:** Dragari
- **Apex Creature:** Auruch (giant sea beings)
- **Key Flora:** Sea Marsh
- **Features:** The most ancient biome. Isolationist. Oral history culture. Legends of sea monsters originate here (actually Dragari).

### 4. The Flickermarch

<picture><source srcset="/static/images/biome-flickermarch.webp" type="image/webp" /><source srcset="/static/images/biome-flickermarch.jpg" type="image/jpeg" /><img src="/static/images/biome-flickermarch.png" alt="The Flickermarch landscape" class="creature-portrait" style="max-width:100%;width:100%;height:auto;border-radius:12px;margin-bottom:20px;border:2px solid #333;box-shadow:0 4px 20px rgba(0,0,0,0.3);"></picture>

- **Type:** Marshland
- **Inhabitants:** Threx
- **Features:** Humid, treacherous terrain. The Threx are rarely seen by outsiders.

### 5. The Tidepools

<picture><source srcset="/static/images/biome-tidepools.webp" type="image/webp" /><source srcset="/static/images/biome-tidepools.jpg" type="image/jpeg" /><img src="/static/images/biome-tidepools.png" alt="The Tidepools landscape" class="creature-portrait" style="max-width:100%;width:100%;height:auto;border-radius:12px;margin-bottom:20px;border:2px solid #333;box-shadow:0 4px 20px rgba(0,0,0,0.3);"></picture>

- **Type:** Coastal shallows, intertidal zones
- **Inhabitants:** Veylar
- **Features:** The only race never bested by Wengari. Deep oceanic knowledge. Isolationist.

### 6. The Underground

<picture><source srcset="/static/images/biome-underground.webp" type="image/webp" /><source srcset="/static/images/biome-underground.jpg" type="image/jpeg" /><img src="/static/images/biome-underground.png" alt="The Underground landscape" class="creature-portrait" style="max-width:100%;width:100%;height:auto;border-radius:12px;margin-bottom:20px;border:2px solid #333;box-shadow:0 4px 20px rgba(0,0,0,0.3);"></picture>

- **Type:** Subterranean networks spanning the entire globe, including beneath oceans
- **Inhabitants:** Chi'Thak
- **Features:** Vast tunnel networks. Cities with glowing stone murals. The Chi'Thak emerge only during Convergences. Largely unexplored and unmapped by surface races.

---

## Oceanic Domain

The world-ocean remains **largely unexplored**. There is speculation of an as-yet-undiscovered sentient race beneath the waves. The Veylar possess the deepest knowledge of the oceans, but even they do not claim to know all its secrets. Deep-ocean exploration is one of the great frontiers of Ethra.
