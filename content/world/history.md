## The Crimson Tide Cycle

- **Period:** 217 years
- **Effect:** Climate disruption for one generation (~25-30 years)
- **Survival:** Requires preparation, stored resources, and often inter-racial cooperation
- **Extinctions:** Each Tide winnows species, races, and civilizations that failed to prepare
- **Resonance:** Maximum magical energy during the Tide's peak

## The Five Tyrants

The term "Tyrant" on Ethra does not necessarily mean "evil ruler." A Tyrant is a being of such immense power and influence that their existence reshapes the world — often catastrophically. Tyrants are products of the Crimson Tide and Convergence.

### The First Tyrant
*(Wengari, Shadow Paw)* — Unified 20 families. Ruled 300 years. Descended into genocidal paranoia as his second Tide approached. Made a "wound so deep" that to this day, other races kill Wengari infants on sight in some regions.

### The Second Tyrant — Crr'zzak "The Fear on Wings"
*(Chi'Thak)* — Lived for only one Convergence. Organized the Chi'Thak blight, devouring across the continent in weeks. Sacrificed himself to feed the colony, spurring species evolution. To the Chi'Thak, a hero. To everyone else, a natural catastrophe.

### The Third Tyrant — Uthgar Lightbringer
*(Wengari, Bright Paw)* — Great administrator and builder. Advanced Sorcery for the Bright Paws. His desert cities now lie in ruins, but his royal legacy endures through the Bright Paw bloodline's hard-light Sorcery.

### The Fourth Tyrant — Xal'thyra the Tidebreaker
*(Veylar)* — A Shell-Singer of extraordinary power, born during a Convergence and touched by both suns. She was the first Veylar to achieve perfect Resonance with all three super-organisms simultaneously — the Kyre Tree, the Mycelial Deep, and the Abyssal Heart. For a century she was a healer, mediator, and scholar who brokered peace between Veylar and Wengari and taught the Pyrinae resonant harmonics.

Then she heard a discord in the world's song that no one else could hear — something buried deep beneath the waves, older than the super-organisms. She became convinced the only way to correct it was to silence every other voice.

Her method was not fire or plague. It was **silence**. She used her Resonance to dampen the Aura of every living thing within her reach, slowly, inexorably. Entire coastlines fell quiet. Cities of the Veylar, Hummans, and Pyrinae were reduced to ghost towns — their inhabitants alive but empty, their inner songs extinguished. She called it the **Great Stillness**.

Her city, **Vey'sul**, now stands as a wound on the world — a place of frozen time, the Veylar haunted by her legacy much as the Hummans are haunted by the Fifth Tyrant's Plague.

### The Fifth Tyrant — Velarius Vane
*(Humman)* — A 65-year plague. Slow, mutating, impossible to defend against. Immunity was impossible; death was excruciating. The most recent Tyrant, and the only one whose shadow still darkens living memory.

## Ruins & Lost Civilizations

The supercontinent is dotted with ruins — empires that failed to maintain the balance between Aura and Sorcery, between the suns, between preparation and complacency. Notable ruins:
- **The Third Tyrant's desert cities** — now partially rehabilitated by Pyrinae builders
- **Pre-Wengari structures** — predating known sentient history
- **Vey'sul** — the Fourth Tyrant's frozen city, a place of the Great Stillness
- **Chi'Thak underground cities** — vast glowing murals depicting super-organisms
- **The Wound** — a scar left by the First Tyrant, location and nature unknown
