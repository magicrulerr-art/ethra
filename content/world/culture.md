## Language

- **Wengari:** Latin (Vulgate) for common use; High Wengari spoken by the Bright Paw capital
- **Pyrinae:** Greek-inspired
- **Other races:** Each maintains their own linguistic traditions

---

## Trade

- **Pyrinae → All:** Rune-glass, Styx feathers
- **Hummans → All:** Pearly Scorpion mounts and eggs
- **Dragari:** Minimal trade; isolationist

---

## Society

Wengari society is clan-based, organized around the five surviving families. Each family maintains its own territory, traditions, and political alliances. The Bright Paws, though diminished, hold the throne by ancient right — their royal bloodline's hard-light Sorcery a mark of the Third Tyrant's legacy.

The Pyrinae live in integrated communities around Kyre Trees, their society built on scholarship and astronomy. They value knowledge above martial strength and serve as the Wengari's closest allies.

Hummans are the youngest race, their society still defining itself. They are industrious, diplomatic, and haunted by the memory of their Tyrant. Their cities trade with all races, making them the economic backbone of the continent.

The Dragari, Veylar, Threx, and Chi'Thak remain largely separate from surface society — by choice, by geography, or by necessity.
